WPCRM Offline

Android Preview 32. English speech recognition is included in the installation. No account or connection is needed to record calls.

Choose speech mode

Normally, tap a field to type or use the microphone on your phone keyboard. The app does not provide online speech. For local speech without internet, check Offline voice recognition; tapping a field then starts the app's on-device microphone. This option starts unchecked on each launch. Both date/time fields start at the current time for each new entry and can be edited manually.

Enter a call

  1. Allow microphone access when asked.
  2. Tap a field and speak. In on-device mode, pauses do not close the microphone.
  3. Press Stop, wait for the last words, and review the text.
  4. Complete the other fields and press Save.

Keep the app open. Switching apps, locking the phone, or a phone interruption can stop capture. Tap the field to resume. Turn Dictate on field tap off to type.

Bring over existing calls

The old browser app has separate storage. Export JSON there, then use Import saved calls here. Import your contact list separately. Keep the old app and backup until you verify your records arrived.

Return to the office

Tap Export JSON and choose a location in Files. The dated file can be transferred by USB or shared when a connection is available. Share JSON opens Android sharing. Review the file in the WPCRM upload chat before CRM entry.

Privacy and backups

Optional on-device speech processes audio in memory locally, without saving it. Your phone keyboard's dictation has its own privacy and connectivity requirements. The app has no INTERNET permission and blocks remote WebView requests. Records remain on this device until you export or share them. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes saved calls and contacts. Export backups regularly.

Updates

Check for updates opens GitHub in your browser, which requires a connection. Download and install the newer signed APK over this app; do not uninstall first. This Android APK cannot be installed on an iPhone.
