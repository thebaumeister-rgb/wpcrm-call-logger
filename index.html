Dictation guide

This guide helps you follow a format while dictating.

  1. Name [Name(s) of the contact(s)]
  2. Subject [Brief description of the call]
  3. Time ["Now" sets start and end to the current time; "Now -3" sets start three hours ago and end now; or specific start and end date and time]
  4. Status ["Open" or "Completed"]
  5. Purpose ["Call" or "Meeting"]
  6. Mileage ["123" or "0"]
  7. Notes [Free notes]
